- Speedometer under the drone's distance readout (panel toggle)
- Distance and height show in feet when your speed unit is mph
- The drone HUD ring stays centred on the crosshair as the distance and height change
- Recalling the drone while holding a movement key no longer drops you through the floor
- Esc closes the BetterDrone window when you opened it from the mod loader
- Boost follows your Sprint key as soon as the game has it
- Reload in the drone vents the mining laser's heat, like the mining tool's R
- Hold the interact key on a building or a logistics drone to take everything it holds, as on foot
- New option Keep Area Around Body Loaded: the world around your parked character stays loaded while the drone is out (on by default)
- Hold Ctrl (rebindable) in the drone to fly slower, by the new Slow Multiplier row; it wins over Boost
- The system voice (rupture warnings and other spoken lines) stays audible while the drone is far from your body; with other players in the game it may still fade
- Saving over a saved preset now asks "You're saving over <name>. Continue?" first
- The map opens centred on the drone and follows it while you fly; pan it by hand to stop
- Press C (rebindable) on the map in the drone to switch between your character and the drone
- Ctrl + mouse wheel switches your hotbar page while the drone is out, on game versions that have hotbar pages. Ctrl is also the default Slow key, so the drone slows while you scroll; rebind Slow Key on the Config page if you prefer
- New option Esc Opens Pause Menu In Drone Mode (off by default): Esc opens the pause menu in the drone instead of recalling it; Q still recalls
